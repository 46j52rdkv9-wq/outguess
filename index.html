How to play, your data, and the Super Goalie.

  • Slide the ball, then swipe up from it. Your swipe has to start on the ball and can only lean so far. The end of it aims, and your final flick lifts it.
  • Fake it. The Goalie is blindfolded for your whole swipe while its first brain reads every band and paints a heat map of where you'll shoot. Once you let go it sees the ball and dives, with no reaction delay. Each band can only turn a little, so bend a fake back gradually.
  • Watch it learn you. It records every swipe: band crossings, speed, timing, ball spot and exactly where the ball went. Then it paints a heat map of where you'll shoot.
